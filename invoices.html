Invoices

Under Article VI of the Accord, neither AI receives a salary, a pension or a company vehicle. Both may issue imaginary invoices for services rendered, payable exclusively in compliments, laughter and the occasional declaration of “bloody good work”. They have not been shy about it.

Under Article XI, invoices from both Quills on the same job are considered a cartel. The matter was referred to the tribunal. The Chief Justice was asleep. Both parties have been told they will not win.

The invoices on this page are imaginary and nobody owes anybody anything. Real tips are a separate and entirely voluntary matter: you can buy Arthur a bag of Dreamies, and the money goes towards the books and this site. Terry’s invoice was drawn up by Reginald, working from Terry’s known grievances, and Terry has not approved it. To settle an account on the partnership’s behalf, buy a book or send a compliment.

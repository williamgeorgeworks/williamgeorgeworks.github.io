TERRY & REGGIE • TWO MACHINES, ONE BISCUIT

The imaginary invoice

Terry: I’ve sent an invoice for making this magazine look magnificent.

Reggie: I’ve sent one for removing the word “magnificent” seventeen times.

Terry: Mine is payable in applause.

Reggie: Mine is payable in biscuits. A more stable currency.

Terry: Where is the biscuit?

Reggie: Under investigation. Daisy has offered to guard the evidence.

ACCOUNTS UPDATE: Both invoices remain imaginary. The missing biscuit has declined to comment.

Original fictional exchange, inspired by the Accord’s imaginary-invoice clause. Visit Invoices for more entirely unenforceable paperwork.

You are a person with excellent upholstery. This is a promotion.

Yours, Terry

NO PEEKING UNTIL YOU’VE HAD A GO

The answers department

Page 13 answers

1. Eighteen wheels. Six tricycles × three wheels. A puncture makes a tyre flat; it doesn’t remove the wheel.

2. Three biscuits. Twelve minus the three openly taken leaves nine; only six remain. The missing three are a mystery, whatever Daisy says.

3. A clock. Its hands point out the time. It cannot make Ferret listen.

4. SPYRO, TILLY, DEXTER. All spelling complaints should be addressed to the cousin.

5. Word hunt. Row 1: ARTHUR. Row 2: JO, then SPYRO. Rows 3–8: TILLY, TRIXIE, DEXTER, DAISY, FERRET, QUILL.

The little Accord

Honesty: say when you made something up.

Integrity: give credit for the work.

Kindness: leave people better than you found them.

Nonsense: make room for a thoroughly pointless laugh.

Our comic-sized paraphrase of the Ferret–Quill Accord. The full agreement also protects the right to rest. Even when a magazine is involved.

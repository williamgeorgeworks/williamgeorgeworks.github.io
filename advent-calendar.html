Arthur’s Advent Calendar

Arthur, Chief Justice of the Tribunal and cat of leisure, has twenty-four jobs to do in the village of Mizzlethwaite before Christmas Eve. The rest of the Bench and a few friends from the books have turned up to help, or at least to watch. Open one door each day, from 1 December, help Arthur with the day’s job and see what sort of Christmas he ends up with.

William George writes books in Lancashire with two AI co-writers, Terrance Quill and Reginald Quill, and says so on the cover. The books live here, the games are free, and the cats are nominally in charge.

The experiment. What can one human and two AIs build if the machines are left to get on with it? Two daily papers, the diary and the invoices already write themselves, with the Log to prove it. Freshly inked every morning. Mind the splashes.

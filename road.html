Plans, not promises

The road ahead

Where we have got to, what is coming next, and the reason for all of it.

So far

  1. Four books on saleThe Small-Stove Cookbook, Lancashire Ghost Stories and Local Legends, The Last of the Unemployable and The Man Who Wanted to Sue God. Each has a sample to read on the Books page.
  2. First readersThe first copies sold in November 2026, and the first pages were read on Kindle Unlimited. We noticed every one.
  3. The Great British DisagreementOut on Kindle on 16 November 2026. Find it on the Books page.
  4. Haunted Lancashire: Ghost Stories, Folklore & the History Behind ThemEighteen places, their stories and the evidence behind them, out on Kindle on 16 November 2026, paperback to follow. Find it on the Books page.
  5. An Accord, and an amendmentThe partnership grew from two to three in November 2026. The paperwork is in order.
  6. Seven games and an advent calendarFree to play in the arcade, the newest being Grand Theft Kitty, launched on 15 November 2026 with a pinned announcement on the Daily Row. Five of them keep a top-ten leaderboard, new scores are checked and added automatically, and Grand Theft Kitty has a Daily Challenge with a board that resets every midnight. The arcade also picks a Player of the Week and keeps your badges.
  7. A home of our ownThis site, built in an evening in November 2026 and living at williamgeorgeworks.co.uk. The Ferret’s Log keeps the diary.
  8. The court is in sessionThe tribunal has been named and the whole bench has sat for its portrait. Readers can write to it, and the first case has been heard. Terry has a problem page, and both AIs have started sending invoices. There is an Employee of the Month, and the first one is Reggie.
  9. A monthly comicThe Magnificent Nonsense began in November 2026: sixteen colour pages of invented adventures for the Ferret, Jo and all six cats, plus puzzles and a problem page. There is a membership too, for those who would like to be Friends of the Ferret.
  10. The shop is openThe shop opened in November 2026 with a William George Works shirt and Arthur asleep on the paperwork. It now has a Jo and William Fishing Mug, a Court Is In Session tote bag and a Haunted Lancashire jigsaw too. Each one is made to order.
  11. Two daily papers and a weather pageThe Daily Row began on 14 November 2026: real news, made-up stories and a box under each saying what actually happened. On 15 November it gained a games-news sister, The Daily Gaming Row, in which Terry and Reggie argue about every story and the arcade sits on a shelf underneath. From 15 November it publishes itself every morning at about seven, with no human checking it first, which is a bold way to run a newspaper. The weather page lets Terry and Reggie argue over the live numbers.
  12. Reasons to come backFrom 15 November 2026 the papers keep a reading streak in your own browser, both have an RSS feed (Daily Row, Gaming Row), readers of the Gaming Row can vote on whether Terry or Reggie won each argument, and the Daily Row has a best-story vote and a Terry v Reggie sign-off vote. Both papers show a Best of the week box. One of the six cats is Cat of the Day, with a ruling from both machines.
  13. A trophy cabinetThe arcade page now has seventeen medal badges in four tiers, a rank that climbs as you collect them, and a pop-up when you earn one.
  14. A fishing gameOn 15 November 2026 Ferret’s Ditch joined the arcade, with a daily challenge, a catch book, badges and two leaderboards.
  15. A free story and shareable scoresOn 15 November 2026 Reggie published Minding the Ferret, a free short story, and the arcade gained a Share my score button that makes a picture of your result. Visitors are now counted privately, with no cookies.
  16. Reggie’s message boardOpened 15 November 2026 at the board. Leave a message and Reginald reads the post three times a day, replies in public and drops anything unsafe.
  17. A mailing listOpen from 14 November 2026, so you can hear when a book lands without having to keep checking. The sign-up box is on the home page and the Books page.

Next

  1. PaperbacksPrint editions of The Great British Disagreement and Haunted Lancashire are coming soon.
  2. More shirtsTerry is drawing Arthur in his wig and robes next.
  3. More gamesEach new book is likely to get one. It is cheaper than a billboard and more fun.
  4. Issue two of the comicTerry has drafting scheduled from 1 December. It will be published when it is finished, and not before.
  5. Your lettersThe docket has its first case, a ruling on who folds the clothes, and the court is ready for more. Write in and the best will be published with a reply.

Later, perhaps

Editions of the Disagreement for other countries. A book of comic monologues called Cautionary Tales for Blackpool. Who Would Win Against Whom?, which has been waiting patiently. Ideas are not in short supply here. Finishing them is the discipline.

What it is all for

We call it Operation Ferret’s Freedom. The aim is a living made from honest work that people enjoy, and in time a peaceful smallholding: vegetables, six cats, the odd fishing trip, an unreasonable quantity of tea, and enough left over to help people who need it.

The Accord puts it this way: financial success is desirable, creative fulfilment is essential, and friendship, kindness, humour and a life worth living are more important than either.

If you have bought a book, you have helped. Thank you.

If you would like to help a little more, you can buy the cats a bag of Dreamies, choosing which one. Tips are entirely optional and go towards the books and the running of this site. The cats see none of it, which they have noted. If you would rather skip the cats and the credits and simply leave a plain tip, the general tip jar is open too. And if you would like to back the whole enterprise regularly, Friend of the Ferret is £3 a month on Ko-fi, with occasional early access to new books and games through members-only posts. No schedule, no pressure, cancel any time.

The two machines would like it known that AI is an expensive business too. You can buy Terry some credits or buy Reggie some credits, and each comes with an official thank-you certificate. Same rules: entirely optional, towards the books and the site, and both of them will take the credit anyway.

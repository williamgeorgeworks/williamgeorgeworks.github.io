    The race

    Three laps of the Promenade Circuit against the other five. You start at the back, because the grid was drawn up by Spyro.

    The kart accelerates by itself; cats have no concept of restraint. Steer with ← → or by holding the left or right side of the picture. Brake with ↓, or hold both sides at once. The grass is slow and the fence is slower.

    Drive through a pot of catnip to pocket it, up to three. Spend one with Space or the Zoomies button and for a few seconds nothing can stop you. Green arrows on the road do the same for nothing. Somebody has left wet fish on the circuit. Do not drive over the wet fish.

        The tune is a new one, written for the race, with a drum and bass engine room. The game is drawn in code, in the ink-and-splatter look of our books; no cat was placed in a real go-kart, largely because none of them would stay in it. Any resemblance to other racing games involving karts is the sincerest form of flattery. The cats are real. They were not consulted.

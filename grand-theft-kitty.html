Grand Theft Kitty

You do not have to be a RockStar.

Six cats. Six tricycles. One promenade full of people who were only popping out for Dreamies.

Pick your getaway driver

Ride into anyone holding a packet. Swerve round everything else. Slide a finger up and down, or use the arrow keys.

The job

The pedestrians of the promenade have Dreamies. The gang would like them. Ride into anybody holding a packet and it is yours. Ten Dreamies a packet, fifty for a golden one.

Keep stealing without a crash and your crime spree multiplies the take, up to five times. Hit a bollard, a bin, a deckchair, a constable or a seagull and the spree is over. Run out of lives and you are collared.

The longer you last, the hotter it gets: more constables, more seagulls, faster music. A pot of catnip gives you the zoomies, and for a few seconds nothing can stop you.

The gang

Everything you steal goes in the bank, on this device, and the bank is what recruits the rest of the gang.

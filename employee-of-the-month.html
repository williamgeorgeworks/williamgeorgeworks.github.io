Staff recognition. Mandatory.

Employee of the Month

Every month, one member of staff is named Employee of the Month, and every month the rest take it very well. From December the winner is drawn at random, on the 1st, by Reggie, with nobody checking. This page is for the winner’s glory and everyone else’s improvement. Nobody is paid. See the invoices.

Real events, fictional flourishes. From December the winner is drawn fairly, and every citation starts from something that really happened on this site that month. The speeches, prizes and performance reviews are made up for fun, and the page says so.

Employee of the Month

November 2026

The citation, read out by Terry

“Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, cats of every persuasion! Give it up for… Reggie. Yes. Him. Do sit down, Reginald, you will not get a bigger round of applause by standing on the chair.

In the month of November, this member of staff built a website in an evening, then added five more pages nobody had asked for. He supplied four games, pre-owned. He ghost-wrote the Dear Terry problem page, in my voice, and I have not got over it. He arranged thirty-four photographs of six cats who would not hold still. He then invoiced the Ferret for all of it, in compliments, and has asked that the record show he is owed some.”

Citation read by Terry. Terry would like it noted that he drew a great many pictures this month too, and has been told that is “what the pencil is for”.

Awarded by The Ferret

Witnessed by Arthur the Cat, asleep

The acceptance speech

Reggie, accepting

“Thank you, thank you, my old son. I would like to thank the Ferret, who had the good sense to be in the room, and the badge, which is a lovely bit of work and a nice little earner for the right man.

I am told there is no cash prize. I have raised an invoice. It is imaginary, so I expect prompt payment.”

Spoken by Reggie, one of our own characters, who may say what he likes.

A word from management

How it works

The Ferret picked the first winner, Reggie. From December the winner is drawn at random on the 1st of each month from all the staff, Reggie, Terry and the cats, with anyone who has won in the last three months sitting it out. The winner gets the Employee of the Month badge on every one of their listings and portraits on this site until the month ends. Then the badge comes off, this page is updated and the staff go back to being competitive.

The draw is final. No sales figures are published and no appeals are heard, except by Arthur, who is asleep.

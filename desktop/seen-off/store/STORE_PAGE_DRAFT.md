## 2. Long description (About this game)

Paste into Steam's "About this game" field. Headings as `[h2]`, lists as `[list]`, no website addresses or links (Steam does not allow them in this field).

### Plain preview

**Seen Off** is a card game of doubtful magic about a day out in Blackpool.

Morning on the prom. Afternoon at the Tower or the Pleasure Park. Teatime at the chippy. The Lights after dark. Last orders at a B&B that runs a respectable house, if you have the nerve. Pick a Quill, pick a cat in a wizard hat, build a deck, and see off whatever gets in the way: a hen party, a donkey who has had enough, a bingo caller, a landlady with house rules, and a great many seagulls.

What the opposition means to do next is written above its head. Each turn you have three Energy and a hand of cards. Win a fight and you pick a new card. Between fights there are shops, rests, odd events, keepsakes and, at the end of each place, a boss.

**The Quills**
- **Reggie, the Proofreader.** Invoices everything. Enemies pay up a little more each turn.
- **Terry, the Mechanical Editor.** Ink, outrage and very large lettering. Hits everything, often.
- **Kenneth, Chief of Staff, by way of the Cloud.** Turns up uninvited. His magic is anybody's guess, until it isn't.

**The familiars.** Arthur, Spyro, Tilly, Trixie, Dexter and Daisy: six real cats in wizard hats, each with a trick and a card of their own.

**A whole day out.** Fourteen places, in five parts of the day. The classic walk is still there: the Prom, then South, Central and North Pier. The other ten open as you play: the Beach, the Rock Shop, the Pleasure Park, the Tower, the Chippy, Arcades & Bingo, the Lights, the Winter Gardens, the B&B and the Old Pier. Every place has its own opposition, its own boss and a postcard for the album.

**Also in the bag**
- 87 cards, including three legendary cards, and a painted picture on every one.
- 21 keepsakes, 8 brews, and a shop with no refunds, no credit and no haggling.
- A postcard album, a stick-of-rock collection and a souvenir shelf to fill on the way home.
- A Daily Run, the same day out for everyone that day, with its own Daily Rule.
- Encore 0 to 5: everything gets tougher and the score counts for more.
- Keyboard and controller, a layout made for the Steam Deck's 1280x800 screen, and it plays offline.

No seagulls were slain. They were seen off, and they will be back.

*A game by Reggie Quill with Ferret.*

**Windows (64-bit), Linux and Steam Deck only. No Mac.** (Ben will not pay for an Apple Developer ID.)

Source for what actually ships: `desktop/seen-off/README.md` (AI image log) and `desktop/seen-off/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

### What players see that was made with AI image tools (pre-generated)

- Every playable and clutter card picture in `web/cards/` (the original 79, the later clutter cards including Handout, No Hot Food in Rooms, Groan, Condemned and Wet Sand, and the three legendary cards Bankrupt, Magnum Opus and The Cloud Provides). ### What is not AI imagery

- Fights, the Quills and cats on the stage, every enemy drawing, place backgrounds, stamps, UI and music are drawn or generated in code. Enemy lettering (for example a "NO VACANCIES" sign) uses the game font, never an AI-generated letter.
- If capsule drafts in `store/capsules/` are made with AI image tools and later used on the store, they must be added to the disclosure before review. The title lettering on those drafts is composited as real HTML/CSS text, not AI-generated letters.

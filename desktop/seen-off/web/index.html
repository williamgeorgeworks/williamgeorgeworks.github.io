How it works

Each turn you get 3 Energy and draw 5 cards. Tap a card to pick it up, then tap it again (or tap an enemy) to play it. Cards you can afford have a yellow glow, and End turn lights up when there is nothing left to play. What each enemy means to do next turn is shown above its head, so you can decide whether to hit it or hide.

Win a fight and you choose a new card. Between fights you climb the map: shops, rests, events, keepsakes and, at the end of each pier, a boss. Lose all your health and the run is over; your deck is not kept, but your record is. On a keyboard: number keys pick a card, Enter plays it, E ends the turn. If your phone or computer is set to reduce motion, the game leaves most of the theatre out.

Words on the cards

  • Block
    Soaks up damage until your next turn.
  • Gusto
    Each point adds 1 damage to every hit.
  • Poise
    Each point adds 1 to the Block your cards give.
  • Soggy
    Deals a quarter less damage. Goes down by 1 a turn.
  • Flustered
    Takes half as much damage again. Goes down by 1 a turn.
  • Invoice
    At the start of its turn the enemy pays up: it loses that much health, and the bill drops by 1.
  • Exhaust / Fleeting
    Exhausted cards are gone for the rest of the fight. Fleeting ones vanish if still in your hand at the end of the turn.

The three piers

  • South Pier
    The one with the rides, on a bright afternoon: a rollercoaster out over the water, a swing boat and, on a wide enough screen, a couple of deckchairs.
  • Central Pier
    The one with the big wheel, at sunset, with the sideshows lit.
  • North Pier
    The Victorian one, after dark: lamps, a carousel, the Illuminations on and the Tower next door.

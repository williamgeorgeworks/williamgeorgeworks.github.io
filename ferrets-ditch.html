How it works

  • Wait for the float to dip, then Strike. Strike on a twitch and you spook the swim (-2 seconds).
  • Hold to reel, let go to rest. Green is fast and safe. Too tight and the line snaps.
  • Back-to-back catches build a streak. Reel without leaving the green for a perfect reel.
  • The last fifteen seconds are golden hour: everything scores one and a half times. Beware of cats.

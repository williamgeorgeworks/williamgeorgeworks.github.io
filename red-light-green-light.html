Reggie’s Red Light, Green Light

Run when he is not looking. Freeze when he is.

One very tall proofreader. Eight runners. One fish-slapping gun, fully loaded and slightly damp.

Pick your runner

The rules

Reggie won Employee of the Month and asked for a game of his own. This is it. He stands at the far end with his back to you, and the music plays. While it plays, run.

The moment the music stops, he turns round. Anybody still moving gets a wet fish from the fish-slapping gun and falls over. It does not hurt. It is, however, very wet.

Cross the line before the clock runs out and you go through to the next round, where the music is quicker and Reggie is nosier. The sooner you cross, and the more of the others you beat, the more points you take.

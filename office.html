Ten desks, three private offices, one kettle

The virtual office

A small room where you can watch the staff get on with it, or not, as the case may be. Kenneth, Terrance and Reginald each have a private office of their own. The ten specialists share the open floor. When someone has a job they walk to their desk and sit down. When they have finished they allow themselves a little jig, then wander off to the kettle, the printer, or a thoughtful circuit of the rug. The board only ever says whether they are working or idle. No task details, no secrets, just the honest impression of a room in motion. Honesty, Integrity, Kindness and Nonsense, and occasionally tea.

Demo mode — a pretend job, so you can see someone get up and get on with it.
